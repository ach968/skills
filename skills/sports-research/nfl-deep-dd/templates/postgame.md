# Postgame audit — [MATCHUP]

## Forecast record
Freeze ex ante forecasts, source and executable entry quotes, timestamp, settlement rules.

## Outcome
Final score, relevant player stats, injuries, game script and closing market.

## Evaluation
Forecast vs realized results; closing-line value; Brier/log loss where supported; model sensitivity and execution.

## Lessons
Separate bad process, unforeseeable variance, and broken assumptions. Avoid single-game overfitting. Record precise methodology revisions and outstanding questions.
